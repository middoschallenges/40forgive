Frequently asked questions

What is 40 Days to Forgiveness?
A free 40-day program that provides 1 quick practical challenge each day to help you let go of hurt, find peace, and strengthen your relationships.
Who is this program for?
Anyone who wants to become more forgiving, whether you're working through a major hurt or simply trying to be a kinder, calmer person.
When does the challenge begin?
We're putting the finishing touches on the daily email series now. Sign up today and Day 1 will land in your email as soon as it's ready — then you'll get one new challenge a day for 40 days.
How much time does it take each day?
Most daily challenges take just 1–3 minutes to read and complete!
What if I'm not sure I can forgive?
That's okay. You don't need to have everything figured out before you begin. Just come with an open mind and a willingness to take one small step each day.
How do I earn tickets for the $100 Amazon gift card raffle?
You'll earn 1 raffle ticket for each challenge you complete. Simply submit the quick completion form at the end of each day's challenge, and your ticket will be automatically added to the raffle. The more days you complete, the more chances you'll have to win! Raffle drawings are held every 3-4 months.
Is it okay if I miss a day?
Absolutely. Just continue with the next day's email — there's no need to catch up all at once.
Wow, this is really powerful! How can I take part in this zechus?
Thank you! The program is offered free thanks to generous sponsors. We depend on people like you to sponsor challenges to help keep covering the costs. You can help bring these daily challenges to even more people by sponsoring a day, a week, or another portion of the program. Sponsor a challenge here.
I love this program! How can I help others participate?
The biggest help is sharing it with your family and friends. Every invitation gives someone else the chance to experience the challenge too. Just send them a link to this website so they can sign up!
Can schools, shuls, or organizations participate as a group?
Absolutely! We'd love to have your community join the challenge together.
